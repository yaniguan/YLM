Repository 2-tier architecture & package map +
Tier 1 · src/model.py & irreps_to_text.py
Core YLM model and Options A / B / C cross-attention (channelwise cuts Option C cross-attn params 18.8×, N=15..400 atoms).
Tier 1 · src/prog.py (GroupProgModel)
Neuro-symbolic invariant selector (force, diff, proj, multi-atom group) + exact O(3) vector executor.
Tier 1 · src/ylm_lm.py & checkpoints/generative_v0
Autoregressive YLMCausalLM generating text + <vec> O(3)-equivariant 3D dipole and force vectors, alongside sub-100ms src/api.py.
Tier 2 · src/universe_fm.py & fm_baselines.py
Multi-rank (0e + 0o + 1o + 2e) model plus Uni-Mol, Signed-Vol, GraphMVP, and EquiLLM baselines.
Tier 2 · src/mace_backbone.py & nequip_backbone.py
Pretrained MACE-MP-0, MACE-OFF23, and NequIP / Allegro adapters + OpenMM / LAMMPS live MD hooks.
Tier 2 · src/toolkit/physics_audit.py & ouroboros_bridge.py
64-bit 8-law conservation checker and 2D-to-3D stereo SMILES bridge.
Tier 2 · lean/LabLean/
Standalone Lean 4 protocol language and decidable safety/volume checker (check_sound, run_conserves).
Tier 2 · labledger/
Standalone append-only SHA-256 hash-chained multimodal experiment ledger with training export gates.
Tier 2 · src/token_factory.py & lab/
Verifier-gated QM9 force question factory, LabLean protocol factory, 4-layer SQL gate, and post-training recipes.
