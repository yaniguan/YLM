## Decision 1: Injecting MD Inter-Frame Displacements (`1o`) & `SEGNN` Tensor Decomposition
- **Literature Basis**: `EGNN` (Satorras et al., ICML 2021), `SEGNN` (Brandstetter et al., ICLR 2022), Calegari Andrade et al. (*Chem. Sci.* 2020), `mlcolvar` (Bonati et al., *JCP* 2023), `ZundEig` (2023), Gomez, Thompson & Laage (*Nat. Chem.* 2024), `EquiLLM` (ICML 2025).
- **Decision**:
  1. Add `n_motion_vectors: int = 0` to `Encoder.__init__` and `extra_vectors=None` to `Encoder.forward` (`use_motion_1o: bool = False` in `YLMConfig`), feeding PBC minimum-image inter-frame displacements $\Delta\mathbf{r}_i = \operatorname{MIC}(\mathbf{r}_i(t) - \mathbf{r}_i(t-1), \mathbf{L}) \in 1o$ directly into Layer 0 while preserving `strict=True` parity when disabled.
  2. Decompose $\mathbf{1o}(\mathbf{F}_i) \otimes \mathbf{1o}(\Delta\mathbf{r}_i) = \mathbf{0e}(W_i = \mathbf{F}_i\cdot\Delta\mathbf{r}_i) \oplus \mathbf{1e}(\boldsymbol{\tau}_i = \mathbf{F}_i\times\Delta\mathbf{r}_i) \oplus \mathbf{2e}(\text{kinematic virial shear})$ in `analyze_md_frame_motion`, and track signed Grotthuss proton-sharing coordinate $\delta(t) = d(\text{D--H}^*) - d(\text{A--H}^*)$, smooth rational proton coordination number $C_i(t)$, and `committed_grotthuss_transfer` vs. `frustrated_rattling` in `detect_md_frame_events`.
- **Whitespace Filled**: Overcomes both silent MLIP scalar-energy readout collapse (`NequIP`/`MACE`) and geometry-blind script-wrapper MD agents (`MDCrow`/`AtomAgents`/`MDAgent2`).

## Decision 2: Electrolyte Solvation Asymmetry (`1o`) + Centrosymmetric Crystal Channel (`2e`)
- **Literature Basis**: `SolvationAnalysis` (Cohen & Persson, *JOSS* 2023), Holoubek & Ping Liu (*Nat. Energy* 2021), `CALiSol-23` (*Sci. Data* 2024), `BAMBOO` (2024), `OMol25` (2025), `MACE-MP-0` (2024), `LiTraj` (*npj Comput. Mater.* 2025), `MolSets` (*PRX Energy* 2024), `DiffMix` (*Nat. Commun.* 2024), `Uni-ELF` (2024), `ChatBattery` (2025).
- **Decision**: Combine smooth cosine coordination numbers, continuous soft SSIP/CIP/AGG speciation probabilities ($0e$), ligand-dipole-to-ion radial alignment $\cos\theta_k = \hat{\boldsymbol{\mu}}_k\cdot\hat{\mathbf{r}}_{\text{ion}\to k} \in 0e$ (quantifying ion–dipole locking stiffness), steric/electrostatic `1o` desolvation escape vector, and a **centrosymmetric `2e` quadrupole cage tensor** $\mathbf{Q}_{\text{cage}} = \sum w(r_{ij})(\hat{\mathbf{r}}_{ij}\otimes\hat{\mathbf{r}}_{ij} - \frac{1}{3}\mathbf{I}_3)$ whose minimum-crowding eigenvector $\pm\hat{\mathbf{u}}_{\text{open}}$ identifies open ion-conduction channels even in inversion-symmetric crystals where `1o` polar vectors cancel to $\mathbf{0}$.

## Decision 3: Drug `2e` Planar Aromatic Tensor, HBD/HBA Cones, Ray-Marching Exit Vectors & Linker Bridges
- **Literature Basis**: `ShEPhERD` (Adams et al., ICLR 2025 Oral), `PharmacoNet` (*Chem. Sci.* 2024), `SurfDock` (*Nat. Methods* 2025), `PocketXMol` (*Cell* 2026), `DecompDiff` (ICML 2023), `DeLinker` (*JCIM* 2020), `3DLinker` (ICML 2022), `LinkerNet` (NeurIPS 2023), `DiffLinker` (*Nat. Mach. Intell.* 2024), `3D-MoLM` (ICLR 2024), `BindGPT` (AAAI 2025), `3D-MolT5` (ICLR 2025).
- **Decision**: Represent planar 5/6-membered aromatic rings with sign-ambiguity-free `2e` symmetric traceless tensors $\hat{\mathbf{n}}\otimes\hat{\mathbf{n}} - \frac{1}{3}\mathbf{I}_3$ (+ pocket-oriented `1o` $\pi$-vectors), compute both HBD (`N-H`/`O-H`) and lone-pair bisector HBA (`1o`) pharmacophore cones with pocket H-bond lock detection, infer open-valence heavy-atom exit vectors when hydrogens are omitted, evaluate multi-step vdW ray-marching clearance ($1.5\text{–}4.2\,\text{\AA}$) distinguishing sub-pocket cavities from solvent-exposed channels, and emit multi-fragment Linker/PROTAC bridging quartets when $N_{\text{frag}} \ge 2$.

## Decision 4: Offline-Resilient Dual-Engine Runtime, 3-Tier Physics Labeler & HF `TensorQA` Exporter
- **Decision**: Support packaged `v0` checkpoints + `YLMCausalLM` (`vec_readout=True`), 3-tier physics labeling (`extxyz` -> `xtb` -> `analytical` $O(3)$-covariant pairwise force & Pauling dipole), dual `3Dmol.js` + offline HTML5 `<canvas>` 3D report + `ThreadingHTTPServer` Studio, and `ylm export-hf` (`src/toolkit/hf_export.py`) with `--anonymized` double-blind support and bundled $K=32$ $O(3)$ rotation+reflection evaluation script.
